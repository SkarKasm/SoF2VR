SoF2VR right-handed defaults
============================

Left stick moves. Click it while pushing forward to sprint.
Right stick turns. Push up to jump and down to crouch.
Right trigger fires or confirms a menu choice. Left trigger handles
secondary fire or the nearby charging action. The M4 and OICW require
support grip for secondary fire.
X uses doors and buttons. Y opens objectives when the weapon hand is free.
The left menu button pauses. Hold both stick clicks for about a second
to recenter. F12 also recenters when the game window has focus.

Hold the right grip to retain the weapon. Release it to drop, or release
at the matching holster to stow. Hold left grip near the gun to support
it with two hands. Release to detach. Toggle grip is available in VR OPTIONS.

Holsters and wheel

- Right hip for handguns.
- Left hip for SMGs.
- Rear right shoulder for shotguns, assault rifles and sniper rifles.
- Rear left shoulder for machine guns and grenade or rocket launchers.
- Front right shoulder for grenades.
- Upper left chest for the knife. Grip the exposed handle below its sheath.
- Stomach for spare magazines and shells.

With an empty weapon hand, briefly grip at an occupied slot to draw its
remembered weapon. Keep holding at the slot to open its category wheel.
Aim the physical right stick at an icon and press the weapon-hand trigger
to equip. Release grip while the wheel is open to cancel. Click the right
stick to page through weapons and Quick Save / Quick Load. Quick Load
requires a save. The knife draws directly when you press grip at its slot.

For a pistol and knife together, keep the pistol gripped in the weapon
hand and grip the knife slot with the other hand. The knife trigger flips
its grip. Move the visible blade into the target to deal damage.
Left-handed mode mirrors the slots and weapon/support actions.

Physical reloads

Press A to eject the magazine. Grip a spare from the stomach pouch with
the support hand and feed it into the magazine well. After an empty pistol
reload, use B to release the slide. For an empty rifle, use the offhand
trigger at the charging handle or bolt to cycle the action. To manually
cycle a loaded weapon, hold that trigger at the action and complete a
back-and-forward stroke. Each full loaded cycle discards a round.

For the M590, take a shell from the pouch and feed it into the receiver
underside. Release grip before taking the next shell. When pumping is
needed, hold the support grip and trigger and complete a full stroke.
M60, MM1, RPG and some alternate modes retain native reload behavior.
Grenade throws follow the reticle.

Use the VR OPTIONS menu or the launcher's VR Accessibility & Controls to
change handedness, holster reach, snap or smooth turn, seated mode and
other settings. Save and Apply commits changes in the game menu.
