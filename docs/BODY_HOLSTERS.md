# Body holsters

Current revision: 5 November 2026. See [accessibility and full control instructions](ACCESSIBILITY_AND_HOLSTERS.md) and the [current meshes, wrist joins and validation](HOLSTERS_WRISTS_ACTIONS_2026_11_05.md).

Right-handed layout:

- Front right shoulder: grenades.
- Front left shoulder: fixed knife.
- Right hip: handguns.
- Left hip: SMGs.
- Rear right shoulder: rifles, shotguns and snipers.
- Rear left shoulder: machine guns, grenade launchers and rocket launchers.
- Stomach: magazines or shotgun shells.

Left-handed mode mirrors all weapon slots horizontally. The stomach pouch stays central.

Hold weapon-hand grip at a slot, navigate with the physical right stick, and press weapon-hand trigger to equip. Stick click changes pages; the last page contains Quick Save/Quick Load. Keep grip held to retain the weapon; release to drop. Knife draws directly on grip at its fixed shoulder slot.

The launcher offers adjustable reach, height, hold duration, pressure thresholds and toggle grip. Seated mode raises hip/pouch slots by 20 cm. Slots use a yaw-only head-derived torso frame. Pistols and the knife now have visible holster/sheath meshes; other categories have visible stowed guns without containers. The knife is mounted handle-down and its trigger flips the held grip.

Virtual gameplay/input checks passed; physical headset fit and comfort remain for owner testing.
